CyberFrac (КиберГРП) is a Russian software product for designing, monitoring and optimising hydraulic fracturing treatments. It was created by the Gazprom Neft Science and Technology Centre in a consortium with MIPT, Skoltech, Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University and the Lavrentyev Institute of Hydrodynamics SB RAS. The software is included in the Unified Register of Russian Software.

The Planar3D ILSA module computes the growth of a single planar hydraulic fracture in an elastic medium layered by confining stress: fracture dimensions (length, width, height), conductivity, fluid pressure, and the transport of fluids and proppant along the fracture. A second module based on the same model handles multistage fracturing. The fracture front is located by the implicit level set algorithm (ILSA) with a near-tip asymptotic solution.

The model is developed further in journal papers: a near-tip asymptote that accounts for stress layers (Engineering Fracture Mechanics, 2023), and acceleration methods that speed up simulations 4.2 times on average and up to 14.4 times (Geomechanics for Energy and the Environment, 2026).
