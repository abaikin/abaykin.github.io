PetroWebTools is an online service for hydraulic fracture calculations right in the browser. It was created at the Laboratory of Software Systems for Hydrocarbon Production Optimization of Novosibirsk State University, is free of charge and is intended for research and teaching; access is granted after registration.

The ILSA 1D module is available now: one-dimensional KGD, PKN and radial (penny-shaped) fracture models with the option to simulate fracture closure, with a choice of two computational cores, ILSA or ExpressFrac. SI, Russian field (Инж РФ) and OilField unit systems are supported. Planned additions are the Unified Fracture Design (UFD) model, proppant transport and the Planar3D model. The interface is in Russian.
